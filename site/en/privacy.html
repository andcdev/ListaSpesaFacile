Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

In short. We only process the data needed to make the list work: your account and what you write in your lists. Only the people you choose to share them with can see them. No advertising, no profiling, no sale of data. The servers are in the European Union.

1. Data controller

Email: support@listaspesafacile.com

2. What data we process

Account

  • name, email address and password (stored only in encrypted, non-reversible form);
  • app language, to send you notifications and emails in your language;
  • profile photo, if you choose to upload one;
  • the date and time you accepted this policy and, if you gave it, your consent to the newsletter.

Sign-in with Google or Amazon

If you sign in with one of these services we receive from them your name, your email and an account identifier. We don't receive your password and we don't access any other data in your account.

List content

  • list names, items, quantities, aisles, reminders and the photos you attach;
  • messages and photos in each list's chat, with the time they were received;
  • who you share lists with and with which permissions;
  • the prices you note down for products ("My prices"): only you can see them.

Device and notifications

  • an access token for each phone you use the app on, with the model name, to keep you signed in;
  • the token Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns to your phone, to send you notifications;
  • the notifications we sent you, until you delete them.

Technical data

Like any online service, the servers log the IP address, date and time and requested address of every access. This is used to protect the service from abuse and to fix faults.

Voice dictation

When you dictate an item, speech recognition is done by the Google service installed on your phone. We only receive the recognised text, never the audio.

3. Why we process it

  • To provide the service you asked for by registering: account, lists, sharing, chat, reminders, notifications, password recovery (art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • To send you the newsletter, only if you asked for it when registering: you can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us (consent, art. 6.1.a GDPR).
  • For the security of the service: limiting sign-in attempts, blocking abuse, fixing faults (legitimate interest, art. 6.1.f GDPR).

We don't use your data for advertising or profiling and we don't sell it to anyone.

4. Where it is and who sees it

The data is stored on servers in the European Union, with netcup GmbH (Germany), which acts as our data processor.

A list's content is only seen by its creator and the people it is shared with. Your name, email and profile photo are seen by the people you share a list with.

Some external services only receive what they need for their job:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): delivers notifications to your phone. It receives the device token and the notification text. Google may process this data in the United States, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Google, Amazon: only if you use them to sign in, according to their privacy policies.
  • The email service provider: delivers the password recovery codes.
  • Open Food Facts: branded product suggestions, their photos and information (ingredients, allergens, nutrition facts) are requested by our server, sending only the product name or barcode, never data about you.

5. How long

  • Account and list data are kept as long as you have the account. You can delete it at any time from the Delete account page: the data is erased immediately (within 30 days if you ask by email).
  • A deleted list disappears immediately for everyone, with its items, photos and messages.
  • Password recovery codes expire after 30 minutes.
  • Technical access data is kept as long as needed for security, normally no more than 30 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, receive it in a machine-readable format, or object to its processing. Write to support@listaspesafacile.com: we reply within 30 days.

If you believe your data is not processed lawfully you can lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, or with the authority of your country.

7. This website

The listaspesafacile.com website uses no cookies, has no visit counters and loads nothing from other domains: no third-party fonts, scripts or images. The language of the page is chosen from your browser settings or from the link you click.

8. Minors

The service is not intended for people under 14.

9. Changes

If this policy changes significantly we'll tell you in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version. In case of doubt, the Italian version prevails.
