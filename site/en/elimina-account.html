Delete your account

Lista Spesa Facile — deletion of the account and all its data

Enter the email you use in the app: we'll send you a code to confirm the mailbox is yours. This also works if you sign in with Google or Amazon: in that case use the email of that account.

If is registered, you'll shortly receive a 6-digit code (valid for 30 minutes). Check your spam folder too.

Account deleted. Your data has been erased. You can uninstall the app: if you open it again it will ask you to sign in.

What is deleted

  • the account: name, email, password, language and profile photo;
  • the link with Google or Amazon, if you used it to sign in;
  • the lists you created, with items, photos, reminders and chat, also for the people you shared them with;
  • the shares: you disappear from other people's lists you took part in;
  • the messages and photos you wrote in the chats;
  • the prices you noted down ("My prices");
  • notifications and your phones' sessions, which are signed out.

What remains

The items you added to other people's lists stay in their lists, without your name. Technical access data (IP address, date and time) are deleted automatically, normally within 30 days, as explained in the privacy policy.

Can't receive the code? Write to support@listaspesafacile.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete account": we'll delete it within 30 days.

Only want to remove some data? In the app you can delete a list, its items and photos, your messages, your profile photo and your notifications, without closing the account.
