1. Content belongs to whoever adds it

Lists, items, notes, chat messages, photos, prices and any other content added to the app are entered by users. Whoever adds content is solely responsible for it, including towards the people they share the list with. Lista Spesa Facile does not review content before it is published and does not endorse it; the exception is photos, which go through an automatic check on our servers: those that appear to contain nudity or violence are rejected.

2. What is not allowed

  • illegal, offensive or defamatory content, or content inciting hatred or violence;
  • content that infringes the rights of others, such as photos of people without their consent or copyrighted material;
  • using the app for spam, unsolicited advertising or to harass other people.

3. Reports and removal

If you find content that breaks these terms, you can report the message or the person directly from the app, or write to support@listaspesafacile.com. We review every report: we may remove content and suspend accounts that do not comply with these terms.

You can also block a person: you can no longer share lists with each other, their messages are hidden and you get no notifications from them. You can unblock people at any time from your profile.

By signing up, or by continuing with Google or Amazon, you accept these terms. How we handle your data is explained in the privacy policy.
