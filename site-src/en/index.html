Shopping, together.

A shopping list to share with the people you live with. Whoever adds, whoever ticks off, whoever is already at the supermarket: everyone sees the same list at the same moment.

For Android phones. In Italian, English, French, German and Spanish.
  • 👥

    Shared in real time

    Invite anyone with their email. Every item added or ticked off shows up at once on everyone's phone.

  • 🎙️

    Dictate it

    "Two kilos of apples", "three yogurts": the app understands name and quantity and adds them to the list.

  • 💬

    Chat in the list

    "Shall I get bread too?" A chat for every list, with photos, without switching to other apps.

  • ⏰

    Reminders

    Set when you're going shopping: the reminder goes to you, to the people you share the list with, or to everyone.

  • 🧺

    Sorted by aisle

    Fruit, dairy, frozen food: items group themselves, with quantity, weight and photos.

  • 📤

    Send it to anyone

    Export the list as text for WhatsApp or Telegram, or as a PDF.
